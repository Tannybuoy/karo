The setup
"Live in the future, then build what's missing."
— Paul Graham

The future of work is distributed. What's missing is belonging.

Remote work gave us freedom and took away something we didn't know we'd miss — the feeling of working alongside people. Not meetings. Not Slack. Just someone sitting across from you, heads down, getting things done.

That used to happen naturally. Now it doesn't happen at all.

The solution

Karo matches you with
one person. Once a week.

Same neighbourhood. Same working style. A café suggestion. A 6-hour window. That's it.

1
Set your profile Working style, focus areas, preferred neighbourhoods, how far you'll travel.
2
Get matched every Saturday at 6pm One person, chosen for compatibility. Confirmed or nothing.
3
Coordinate in-app Ephemeral chat to pick a café, agree on a time. No group chats. No noise.
4
Show up. Work. Leave. No agenda. No follow-up required. The value is in the session itself.

B2B
$15/seat

Companies pay to give Karo to remote employees as a benefit. Solves a real HR problem — isolation and retention.
