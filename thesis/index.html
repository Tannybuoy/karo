The venture narrative
Remote work successfully solved the logistics of flexibility. It failed to solve the human need for belonging.

Karo is not another social app. It is infrastructure for distributed work culture — matching remote workers, freelancers, and creators with compatible peers in their neighbourhood for recurring 3-hour work sessions at local cafés.

People solved remote work logistics. They have not solved remote work belonging. Karo is the recurring real-world coordination layer for the modern workforce.
